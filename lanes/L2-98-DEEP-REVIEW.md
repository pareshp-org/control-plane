## 6. Verdict

**BLOCKED.**

1. **The lane cannot be planned.** Three documents each claim to be the executable decomposition of `.github/workflows/**`, and fourteen owned paths are written by two or three of them with truncating redirects under mutually exclusive acceptance criteria (**B1**). No document sequences the lane's 160 bodied tasks, and the one that claims to be complete covers 76 of them (**B2**). Which document survives is an L0 ruling, and nothing downstream can be fixed sensibly before it is made, because roughly a third of the lane's task bodies will be struck by it.

2. **The lane's external contract is forked.** L0 froze an eleven-context required-check list naming L2 as the emitter; L2 publishes fifteen different names, never cites the frozen contract, and leaves two contexts that L5's branch protection requires and no workflow in the estate can emit (**B3**). L0 also byte-freezes `lane-guard.yml`, which two L2 tasks author and edit, invisibly (**B4**). Both are cross-lane and neither is L2's to decide.

3. **Even with the plan settled, execution stops at the first and last command of every task.** The environment variable every block opens with is an unresolved placeholder and no task creates the repository (**B6**); the close block every task ends with neither stages nor commits, so all 70 PRs would be empty (**B7**); and the guard in between fires on legal changes while being vacuous where it matters (**B8**).

4. **Three named phase gates are unpassable as written** — BT-1 (**B11**), BT-3 (**B12**), and the whole of `L2-06` from its first task onward (**B13**) — and twelve individual tasks print `FAIL` on a byte-correct build under STOP rules that forbid the only available fix (**B14**).

5. **The artifacts that would be built are dead on arrival.** The actor gate reads a path L1 never creates (**B17**); the workflow-identity gate cannot authenticate and so refuses every production deploy (**B20**); two workflows do not parse (**B18**); the evidence chain's own closed fixture reports the chain open (**B21**).

6. **The review record cannot be trusted to drive the fix** (§5).

Nothing here is a judgement about quality. Every Tier 0–2 blocker was reproduced with a command, or is a direct textual contradiction between two documents that each declare themselves binding.
